Getting started

What is Calwise?

Calwise is an iPhone app for tracking calories and nutrition, built around Indian food. You can log a meal by typing it (Hinglish is fine), saying it, photographing it, scanning a barcode or searching a catalogue of about 48,700 foods, most of them Indian dishes.

Home shows today's calories against your target, with protein, carbs and fat. Progress shows your week, month and six months, your weight, your streak and a Monthly Wrapped story.

Which iPhones does Calwise work on?

Calwise 2.0 needs iOS 27 or later. iPhones that can't update to iOS 27 can keep using Calwise 1.1, which the App Store offers them automatically.

How does Calwise work out my daily targets?

Your daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets come from your height, weight, age, sex, activity level, goal (lose, keep or gain weight) and pace, using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula.

They're estimates, and they may not suit everyone. When you change any of those details in Profile, Calwise asks whether to work your targets out again. You can also set the targets yourself in Goals.

How can I sign in?

With your email and a password (we email you a 6-digit code to confirm the address), with Sign in with Apple, or with Sign in with Google.

New accounts answer a few questions about you and your goal first, so your targets are ready as soon as you're in.

I forgot my password.
  1. On the Log in screen, tap Forgot Password?
  2. We email a code to the address you typed (or ask for one).
  3. Enter the code, then choose a new password.

No email? Check spam, and make sure it's the address you signed up with. If you use Apple or Google to sign in, sign in that way instead. There's no Calwise password to reset.

Logging meals

What are the ways to log a meal?
  • Type it in plain words, for example “do roti aur ek katori dal”.
  • Say it out loud.
  • Snap it: take or choose a photo of your plate.
  • Scan it: point the camera at a packet's barcode.
  • Search the catalogue for a single food.

Whichever you use, you see each food with its amount and values on a card, and can adjust it before tapping Add.

Can I type in Hinglish?

Yes. Type the meal the way you'd say it: “2 roti, dal tadka aur dahi” or “ek plate poha”. Calwise finds each food and its amount, and the logged meal keeps your words as its title.

Typing a single food that's in the catalogue is matched straight away. Reading a whole meal uses Calwise's optional AI.

How does voice logging work?

Tap Voice and describe your meal. Recording stops after a short pause once you've started speaking, when you tap again, or after one minute.

Your words appear so you can fix anything before tapping Continue. Hindi comes back written in English letters (“do roti…”). If you haven't allowed the microphone, you can type the meal instead.

How does photo logging work?

Tap Camera and take a photo of your plate or choose one. Calwise names each dish with a serving and an estimated weight, then matches each one:

  • to a catalogue food when it's a confident match;
  • otherwise to an AI estimate, which is labelled as one;
  • or to the closest catalogue food, shown by name so a loose match is easy to spot;
  • or Not found, with a Search button.

Check the amounts before you add them. If the photo has a barcode in it, Calwise reads that instead. If it can't see any food, it may be a packet. You can type the barcode number to look it up.

A barcode isn't found.

Calwise checks its own catalogue first, then Open Food Facts, a public database of packaged foods.

  • “Product not found” means neither knows that barcode. Search for the food instead.
  • “Couldn't look up the barcode” means Open Food Facts couldn't be reached (you may be offline). The product may well exist, so try again later.
Can I log in katori, roti or grams?

Yes. When you open a food, the editor offers household units such as katori, roti and piece, as well as grams. A katori is 150 g and a roti is 40 g.

How do saved meals work?

Save meals you eat often and log them again with one tap. Open them from the Log tab's bookmark button. Create one with +, or tap Save as meal in the food editor.

Your three most recent foods also appear under Suggested when the search field is empty, each with a + to log it again at the same amount.

How do I edit or delete a meal?

On Home, touch and hold a food, then choose Edit or Delete. On the Log tab, use a meal card's menu.

Changes reach Apple Health as well, and work offline too. They're sent when you're back online.

AI features

Do I have to use AI?

No. Typed meals, voice and photos use AI, and Calwise asks before your first AI request. Search, barcodes and saved meals all work without it.

You can turn AI features on or off at any time in Profile › AI Features.

What's sent when I use AI?

Only what that request needs: a compressed meal photo, the recording of what you said, or the text you typed. It goes through Calwise's server to OpenAI. Calwise doesn't add your name or email address.

OpenAI may use what you send to improve its models. Section 3 of the privacy policy explains this in full.

“You've reached today's limit for AI”

Each account has a daily allowance for each kind of AI request. Currently that's 20 photos, 40 typed meals and 30 voice logs a day. Allowances reset at midnight UTC (5:30 am in India).

Until then, single foods, search, barcodes and saved meals still work. A typed food that matches the catalogue exactly doesn't use your allowance.

Are AI estimates accurate?

They can be wrong. Portions are especially hard to judge from a photo. Calwise labels AI estimates, and you can change amounts or remove foods before tapping Add. Calwise gives estimates and tracking tools, not medical advice.

Streaks & reminders

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days you logged something. Life happens, so up to two empty days in a row only pause it, shown by a grey pause icon. Log something within those two days and it carries on. A third empty day ends it.

While it's paused, the Progress tile tells you how long you have left, for example “Log today or tomorrow to keep it”. Paused days don't add to the count.

When do reminders arrive, and can I change them?

By default: breakfast at 9:00, lunch at 13:30 and dinner at 20:30, plus a nudge at 20:00 if nothing's been logged that day. Logging a meal removes that day's reminder for it.

Change the times or turn any of them off in Profile › Notifications. Tapping a reminder opens the Log tab, ready for that meal.

My reminders don't arrive.
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Calwise in iPhone Settings › Notifications.
  • Check the reminder is on in Profile › Notifications.
  • Open Calwise now and then. It plans reminders two weeks ahead and refreshes them each time it opens.
What is Monthly Wrapped?

A short story of cards about your month: total meals, favourite foods, your longest streak and how your weight changed. Open it from Progress.

Apple Health

What does Calwise share with Apple Health?

To Health: each meal you log, with its calories, protein, carbs and fat. Editing or deleting a meal updates Health too, without duplicates.

From Health: steps and active energy (shown on Progress for the last 7 days), your latest weight (added to your weight history), and height, date of birth and sex (to fill in your details when you set up).

Apple Health data is never sent to AI providers. The privacy policy has the details.

Does my activity change my calorie target?

No. Steps and active energy appear on Progress for context only. Your calorie target comes from your details and goal.

My meals aren't showing in Apple Health.
  • Check that Calwise is allowed to write nutrition data: Profile › Apple Health in Calwise, or Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Calwise on your iPhone.
  • A meal reaches Health once it has uploaded. If you logged it offline, it appears after Calwise syncs.

Offline & syncing

Can I use Calwise offline?

Mostly, yes. Without a connection you can open Home, today's Log, Progress, Profile and your saved meals, and they show “Offline · Last updated…”. Logging recent foods and saved meals, editing, deleting and changing goals all work and upload later.

These need a connection: search, typed meals, photos, voice, barcodes Calwise hasn't seen before, signing in, and deleting your account.

“Changes waiting to sync” in Profile

Some changes haven't reached our server yet. Calwise retries by itself when you open the app, when the connection returns, and at intervals after that.

If the server refused a change, tap Retry in Profile. Still stuck? Contact us and tell us roughly when it happened.

Will I lose meals if I log out before they've synced?

No. Calwise tries to send them first. Anything it can't send stays on your iPhone, kept for your account, and uploads the next time you sign in on that iPhone.

Account & data

Profile shows no name after Sign in with Apple.

Apple shares your name only the first time you sign in with Apple. Add it yourself in Profile › Account.

How do I log my weight?

On Progress, open the weight tile and tap Log Weight. If Apple Health is connected, your latest weight from Health is added too. When a day has both, the one you entered wins.

How do I log out?

Profile › Log Out, then confirm. Calwise clears your data from this iPhone, including reminders and your AI choice. Anything not yet uploaded is kept for your next sign-in.

How do I delete my account?
  1. Open Profile and tap your name.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more.

Your data is deleted from our server, along with your account, and you're signed out on this iPhone. This can't be undone, and it needs a connection. Meals already saved to Apple Health stay in Health until you remove them there.

Can I get a copy of my data, or ask a privacy question?

Yes. Email calwiseapp@gmail.com from your account's address (or say which address it is). Please don't send passwords or sign-in codes. Section 8 of the privacy policy lists your rights.
