# Calwise Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [RELEASE DATE]
**Last updated:** [RELEASE DATE]
**Applies to:** Calwise for iOS and the Calwise services that link to this Policy

Calwise ("Calwise", "we", "us" or "our") helps you record meals, understand what you eat and follow your progress. This Policy explains what information Calwise uses, why, who else receives it, and the choices you have.

Please read it together with the permission prompts iOS shows and the notice Calwise shows before your first use of its optional AI features. Calwise gives estimates and tracking tools. It is not a medical service and doesn't diagnose or treat anything.

## 1. Information we collect

What Calwise uses depends on the features you choose and the permissions you give.

### 1.1 Account and sign-in
Your email address, display name, account identifier and sign-in session. When you sign in with an email code, the code is emailed to you. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, that provider supplies what is needed to sign you in; Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your email.

### 1.2 Profile, body measurements and goals
To work out your targets: your age, sex, height, current weight, target weight, activity level, weight goal and pace, and the targets calculated from them (daily calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat). You can change these at any time in the app.

### 1.3 Meals and nutrition
The foods you log or save: names, meal, portions and weights, time, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and other nutrients, saved meals and custom foods, and your daily totals, streaks and weight history.

### 1.4 Apple Health (optional)
With your permission, given in Apple's Health permission screen, Calwise reads on your iPhone:
- steps and active energy;
- body weight and height;
- date of birth and sex.

Calwise uses steps and active energy to show your activity on Progress, and height, weight, date of birth and sex to fill in your profile when you set it up. **Your latest Apple Health weight is added to your weight history in Calwise** so Progress can chart it. Nothing else read from Apple Health is uploaded, and Apple Health data is never sent to OpenAI or any other AI provider. Profile values you save (even ones filled in from Apple Health) are stored with your account as profile information.

With your permission, Calwise can also save the meals you log to Apple Health, with their calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat.

### 1.5 Photos, barcodes and voice
- **Camera and photos:** used when you photograph or choose a picture of a meal to log it.
- **Barcodes:** the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product.
- **Microphone:** used to record a meal you describe by voice, for transcription.
- **Profile photo:** if you add one, it stays on your iPhone and isn't uploaded.

Meal photos, recordings and meal descriptions are sent for AI processing only after you agree and only when you use an AI feature (Section 3).

### 1.6 Preferences and service information
Appearance, reminder times, your AI choice, and the copies of your data the app keeps on your iPhone so it works offline. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone, and their settings aren't sent to us. On our server we keep limited operational records, such as how many AI requests you made each day, whether they succeeded and how long they took. We use these to enforce fair daily AI limits, protect the service from abuse and fix problems. They don't contain your photos, recordings or meal descriptions.

## 2. How we use information
- **Your account:** to create it, sign you in, and keep your information with it.
- **Your targets:** to estimate your energy needs and daily calorie and macronutrient targets. These are estimates and may not suit everyone.
- **Your diary and progress:** to total your meals, keep your food diary and history, and show your progress.
- **Storage and sync:** to keep your profile, meals, saved meals, weight history and summaries in your account so they're there when you sign in.
- **Apple Health:** as described in Section 1.4.
- **Optional AI features:** to recognise food in photos, understand meals you type or say, estimate portions and transcribe voice logs, when you choose to use them.
- **Running and protecting the service:** to fix errors, enforce AI limits, prevent misuse and keep accounts secure.
- **Support:** to answer your messages and send important notices about the service.

We don't use your information to make medical decisions about you. AI food recognition, portions and nutrition values can be wrong; check them before you rely on them.

## 3. Optional AI features and OpenAI
Photo logging, voice logging and AI meal descriptions are optional. Calwise asks for your agreement before it sends anything for AI processing. Without it, everything else still works: food search, barcodes and saved meals.

When you use an AI feature, the content for that request goes through Calwise's server to OpenAI: a compressed meal photo, the recording of what you said, or the text you typed (or the text of your recording). Calwise doesn't add your name or email address. A photo, recording or description can still contain personal details, so check what you capture before you send it.

**OpenAI may use what you send to improve its models.** Calwise takes part in OpenAI's data-sharing programme: OpenAI may use the content of Calwise's AI requests, and its replies, to identify usage patterns, measure model quality, and evaluate and train its models. In return Calwise receives free AI usage from OpenAI. OpenAI handles this content under its own policies, including how long it keeps it; see OpenAI's privacy policy and API data controls (links in Section 4). Please don't include anything in a photo, recording or description that you'd rather not share.

You can turn AI features off at any time in Profile › AI Features. That stops future AI requests; it doesn't undo requests already made or change how OpenAI keeps content it has already received.

## 4. Who else receives information
We don't sell your personal information or health information. The Calwise app has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and doesn't share Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.

We use these providers only to run the features you use:

| Provider | What for | What they receive |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Sign-in, the database and Calwise's server functions | Your account, profile, meals, weight history and the requests needed to run the service. Our database is hosted in Mumbai, India. Access to your records is restricted to your account by row-level security. |
| **OpenAI** | Optional AI features (Section 3) | The photo, recording or text for each AI request you make. |
| **Resend** | Sending sign-in codes by email | Your email address and the code. |
| **Open Food Facts** | Packaged-food lookups | The barcode number you scan. |
| **Apple** | Apple Health and Sign in with Apple | Health data you allow Calwise to read or write; Apple's sign-in information. |
| **Google** | Sign in with Google, if you choose it | The sign-in information Google provides. |

Each provider handles information under its own terms: [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) · [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) · [OpenAI API data controls](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) · [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy) · [Open Food Facts terms](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use) · [Apple Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) · [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or when it is reasonably needed to protect people, Calwise or our legal rights.

## 5. Apple Health safeguards
Apple Health access is optional and controlled by iOS. You choose what Calwise may read or write and can change it at any time: open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps (or Apps and Services) › Calwise. Permissions are set separately on each device.

Apple Health data is used only for Calwise's health and fitness features. It isn't used for advertising or marketing, sold, or given to data brokers. Section 1.4 lists what is uploaded (your latest weight only).

Turning off a permission stops future access. It doesn't remove what Calwise already wrote to Apple Health; you can delete those entries in the Health app.

## 6. Storage, security and international transfers
Your account data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India. The app connects over encrypted connections, and every request is checked against your account. Your iPhone also keeps copies of your data and preferences so the app works offline.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no system is completely secure. Protect your device and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

Some providers process information outside India: OpenAI and Resend are based in the United States, and Apple, Google and Open Food Facts operate internationally. Their privacy laws may differ from yours. Where the law requires it, we take steps so that this processing has appropriate safeguards.

## 7. How long we keep information, and deleting your account
We keep your information while you have an account and for as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, to resolve disputes, protect the service, or meet legal obligations.

You can delete your account in the app: Profile › your name at the top › Delete Account. This removes your profile, meals, saved meals, custom foods, weight history and daily summaries from Calwise's database. If you used Sign in with Apple, Calwise also asks Apple to revoke its access.

Some copies may remain for a limited time: in backups, in security and service logs, and with providers under their own retention rules (for OpenAI, see Section 3). Data Calwise wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Data the app keeps on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app.

## 8. Your choices and rights
You can:
- see and change your profile, goals and food records in the app;
- turn AI features on or off in Profile › AI Features;
- allow or remove Apple Health access in the Health app or iOS Settings;
- change reminders in the app and in iOS notification settings;
- delete your account in the app.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your information, a correction or deletion, to withdraw consent, or to raise a concern or complaint. We may need to confirm it's you before we act, and we'll reply as the law requires. If a request can't be met in full, we'll tell you why.

## 9. Children
Calwise is for people aged 18 and over. People under 18 shouldn't create an account or use Calwise. We don't knowingly allow accounts for people under 18. If you think someone under 18 has given us their information, contact us and we'll look into it and act.

## 10. This website
The Calwise app doesn't use cookies, and this website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It's hosted by Vercel.

The home and contact pages use Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use third-party cookies: Vercel tells visits apart by a code worked out from each request, which it discards after 24 hours. With each visit it may record the page, the site you came from, your approximate location, and your browser, operating system and type of device. Vercel says this information isn't tied to you or your IP address and can't be used to follow you across websites ([Vercel Web Analytics privacy](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy)).

The home page also loads its animation code from jsDelivr, a public content network, which receives your IP address the way any website you visit does.
