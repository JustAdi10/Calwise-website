Calwise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026
Last Updated: November 3, 2026
Applies to: Calwise for iOS and the Calwise services that link to this Policy

Calwise ("Calwise," "we," "us," or "our") provides tools for recording meals, understanding nutritional intake, and monitoring nutrition-related progress. We take the privacy of your personal information seriously. This Policy explains what information Calwise processes, why it is used, when it is shared, and the choices available to you.

Please read this Policy together with the permission prompts shown by iOS and any separate consent notice displayed before you use optional AI features. This Policy is not a medical or nutrition-care statement. Calwise provides estimates and tracking tools, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed by Calwise depends on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant.

1.1 Account and sign-in information

When you create an account or sign in, Calwise processes information such as your email address, display name, account identifier, and authentication or session information. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, the relevant provider supplies the authentication information needed to complete sign-in. Apple may provide a private-relay email address instead of your personal email address.

1.2 Profile, body measurements, and nutrition goals

To calculate and personalize nutrition targets, Calwise may process your date of birth or age, biological sex, height, current weight, target weight, activity level, weight goal, and goal pace. It may also process calculated targets such as daily calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, and estimated energy requirements. You can enter or update these details in the app. Where you permit Apple Health access, some profile fields may be pre-filled from HealthKit.

1.3 Meal records and nutrition information

When you log or save food, Calwise processes information such as food names, meal types, portions, serving weights, meal times, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre, recipes, notes, saved meals, and daily summaries. Calwise uses these records to provide your food diary, nutrition totals, history, streaks, and progress features.

1.4 Apple Health information (optional)

If you grant Calwise permission through Apple's HealthKit permission interface, Calwise may read the following information from Apple Health on your device:

  • Step count and active energy burned.
  • Workout information.
  • Body weight and height.
  • Date of birth and biological sex.

Calwise uses activity information for context in the Progress experience and may use height, weight, date of birth, and biological sex to pre-fill profile or onboarding fields. HealthKit read data is queried on your iPhone and, according to Calwise's current implementation, is not uploaded to Calwise's cloud database or sent to OpenAI or other external AI providers. If you choose to save information pre-filled from Apple Health into your Calwise profile, the profile values you save are stored with your Calwise account as profile information.

Calwise may also write the dietary energy consumed for meals you log to Apple Health. This is a write to your Apple Health record; it is separate from the HealthKit information Calwise reads.

1.5 Photos, barcode information, and audio

If you choose image-based food logging, Calwise may access the camera or a photo you select to capture a meal. If you use barcode scanning, the app processes the barcode and may send the numerical barcode value to Open Food Facts to retrieve packaged-food information. If you use voice logging, Calwise accesses the microphone to capture the spoken meal description for transcription. Profile-image features, where enabled, may also process an image you select.

Meal photos, spoken audio, and food-description text are sent for AI processing only when you have given the required consent and actively use the relevant AI feature. More detail is provided in Section 3.

1.6 App preferences and operational information

Calwise may process your measurement preferences (for example, kilograms or pounds), display theme, reminder schedules, AI-consent preference, local offline cache information, and limited operational information such as feature-usage counters and error codes. Feature-usage counters are used to enforce fair daily AI limits and help protect the service from abuse.

2. How We Use Your Information

Calwise uses information for the following purposes:

  • Account access: create and maintain your account, authenticate you, recover access, and keep your information associated with your account.
  • Nutrition calculations: estimate basal metabolic rate, total daily energy expenditure, and daily calorie and macronutrient targets using profile information and your selected activity and weight goals. These calculations are estimates and may not be suitable for every person.
  • Food and progress tracking: calculate meal totals, maintain your food diary, show nutrition history, and present progress information.
  • Cloud synchronization: store your Calwise profile, meal records, saved meals, weight history, and related summaries so that they are available when you sign in on a supported device.
  • Apple Health integration: read the HealthKit information you authorize for the purposes described in Section 1.4 and write dietary energy from logged meals when you authorize that action.
  • Optional AI features: analyze meal images, interpret food descriptions, estimate portions, and transcribe voice meal entries when you choose to use an AI feature and have provided the required consent.
  • Service operation and protection: troubleshoot errors, maintain the app, enforce AI usage limits, prevent misuse, and protect account and service security.
  • Support and communications: respond to questions, feedback, and requests you send to us, and provide important service-related notices.

We do not use your information to make medical diagnoses. AI-generated food recognition, serving-size, and nutrition estimates may be incomplete or inaccurate; review them before relying on them.

3. Optional AI Features and OpenAI Processing

AI-powered image logging, voice logging, and AI-based food-description processing are optional. Calwise requires an affirmative consent action before sending the relevant content for AI processing. You can decline AI processing and continue to use non-AI features such as manual food search, barcode lookup, and saved meals.

When you choose an AI feature, the content needed for that request may be sent through Calwise's authenticated server gateway to OpenAI. Depending on the feature, this may include a compressed meal photograph, an audio recording or audio snippet, or text describing the food. Calwise does not intentionally include your account name or email address in the AI request. However, a photograph, recording, or text that you submit may itself contain personal or identifying information, so review what you capture or say before submitting it.

OpenAI's current API documentation states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve its models by default unless the customer opts in. OpenAI also states that API abuse-monitoring logs may contain customer content and are generally retained for up to 30 days, subject to legal, safety, and other stated exceptions. OpenAI's retention can depend on the endpoint and account configuration; see OpenAI's API data controls for current details.

You can turn off Calwise AI features in Profile → AI Features. Turning AI off prevents future use of the optional AI features, but it does not undo processing that has already taken place or determine the independent retention practices of a third-party provider.

5. Apple HealthKit Safeguards and Your Controls

Apple Health integration is optional and controlled by permissions you grant through iOS. You can choose which categories Calwise may read or write and can change those permissions later. Permissions are managed separately on each device.

HealthKit information is used to provide Calwise's health and fitness features. It is not used for advertising, marketing, sale to data brokers, or unrelated data mining. HealthKit read information is queried on your iPhone. Under the current implementation described in this Policy, it is not uploaded to Calwise's cloud database or sent to OpenAI or other external AI providers.

To review or change Calwise's Health access, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, and open Apps or Apps and Services → Calwise. Menu names can vary by iOS version. You can also review the Health permissions shown in iOS settings. Revoking permission stops future access to the relevant HealthKit category; it does not necessarily remove information already written to Apple Health.

To remove dietary-energy entries previously written by Calwise, manage or delete those records in the Health app. Removing your Calwise account does not automatically guarantee deletion of records that have already been written into Apple Health.

6. Data Storage, Security, and International Processing

Calwise stores account and app data using Supabase-managed backend infrastructure. The app uses authenticated service requests and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies on core user-data tables to restrict access to records. Data transmitted between the app and Calwise services uses encrypted network connections. Local device storage may also be used for preferences and offline caching.

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect information, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should protect your device and account credentials and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy laws in those locations may differ from the laws where you reside. Where required, we take steps intended to ensure that such processing is subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide Calwise, maintain your account, meet the purposes described in this Policy, resolve disputes, protect the service, or comply with applicable law.

You can initiate account deletion in the app through Profile → Delete Account. Calwise's account-deletion workflow is designed to remove your profile, meal logs, saved meals, weight history, and daily summaries from the primary Calwise database. If you used Sign in with Apple, the deletion workflow also requests revocation of the associated Apple authorization.

Limited copies may remain for a period in system backups, security records, or third-party service logs where needed for recovery, security, legal compliance, or the provider's stated retention practices. The exact period can depend on the relevant system or provider. OpenAI's API retention information is described in Section 3.

Data written by Calwise into Apple Health is held in Apple's Health database and must be managed separately in the Health app. Local preferences or cached information on your device may also be subject to the app's local storage and cleanup behavior.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Requests

You can:

  • Review and update profile details, goals, and food records in the app.
  • Turn optional AI features on or off in Profile → AI Features.
  • Grant, restrict, or revoke Apple Health permissions in the Health app or iOS settings.
  • Manage reminders through the app and your device notification settings.
  • Delete your Calwise account through Profile → Delete Account.

You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and explain if a request cannot be completed in full.

Where applicable law gives you additional rights, including rights relating to consent, erasure, or grievance redressal, you may exercise them by contacting us using the details in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

Calwise is intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. Individuals under 18 should not create an account or use Calwise. We do not knowingly offer accounts to individuals under 18. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information to Calwise, contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Website Technologies and Local Storage

The Calwise iOS app uses on-device storage for app preferences, consent settings, and offline caching. Browser cookies are not used by the iOS app. The website may use essential technologies required for hosting, security, or basic site functionality. Any analytics, advertising, or non-essential cookies or similar technologies used by the website should be disclosed separately and controlled as required by applicable law.
