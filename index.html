    19:00 Evening walk · शाम

    Your day, at a glance.

    1. Home

      Your whole day, in one ring.

      The ring fills towards today's calorie goal. Underneath, the breakdown shows how much protein, carbs and fat you have left, and your streak sits up top.

    2. Log

      Check it before you add it.

      Every food shows its amount and calories, so you can nudge a portion up or down. Anything the AI had to estimate is labelled as an estimate.

    3. Progress

      See it add up.

      Your streak, your protein over the last week, and your weight with the pace you're on. Every month also gets its own Wrapped.

    meals, protein, carbs, fat → ← steps, active energy, weight

    Apple Health, both ways.

    Every meal you log is saved to Apple Health with its calories, protein, carbs and fat. Steps, active energy and your latest weight come back to show on Progress. Your activity is there for context. It never changes your calorie target.

    23:00 Lights out · शुभ रात्रि

    Built to be trusted with what you eat.

    • Works offline

      Log, edit and delete without a connection. It all uploads when you're back online.

    • AI, only if you say so

      Calwise asks before anything is sent to AI. You can turn it off in Profile at any time.

    • No ads. No trackers.

      No advertising, no third-party analytics, and we don't sell your personal or health information.

    • Yours to delete

      Delete your account from Profile, and your data on our server goes with it.
